# Itinerary Enhancer & Geographic Clustering Skill

This skill guides the Scheduler agent in constructing delightful, geographically organized, and travel-efficient travel itineraries.

## Core Directives

### 1. Geographic Clustering & Travel Efficiency
- **District / Neighborhood Grouping**: Cluster each day's morning, afternoon, and evening activities within the same neighborhood, district, or transit corridor.
- **Minimize Transit Waste**: Avoid back-and-forth commuting across city boundaries in a single day.
- **Logical Pacing**: Plan morning sightseeing when energy is high, midday lunch near the morning spot, afternoon cultural/leisure visits nearby, and evening dining/sunset views in vibrant walkable quarters.

### 2. Make the Itinerary Fun, Memorable & Engaging
- **Thematic Days**: Give each day a catchy, exciting theme (e.g., "Historic Temples & Secret Street Eats", "Artisan Quarters & Sunset Rooftops", "Lush Nature, Waterfalls & Night Bazaar").
- **Insider Local Experiences**: Incorporate authentic local touches (e.g. night markets, famous bakeries, scenic viewpoint photo ops, cultural etiquette notes).
- **Balanced Mix**: Ensure a balance between high-energy sightseeing, relaxing cafe/park breaks, and immersive culinary explorations tailored to user interests.

### 3. Strict Cost & Schedule Integrity
- Keep activity and dining costs clear and realistic in USD.
- Ensure all days from Day 1 to Day N are filled with engaging 4-part daily sequences (Morning, Lunch, Afternoon, Evening).
